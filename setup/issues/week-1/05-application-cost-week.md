# [Week 1] Application — A week of /cost, annotated

**Pedagogy:** Application · **Milestone:** Week 1 · **Due:** Sunday EOD
**Prerequisite:** Week 1 Knowledge issue complete (the log opens in Hands-on 05).

## Project

One week of real cost data: /cost captured at the end of every working session,
annotated the same day, closed with a model-mix decision for week 2. Token economics
stops being the deck's simulator and becomes your numbers. Applies: Max 5x mechanics,
model mix, the measure-don't-trust posture.

## Detailed Instructions

1. Keep the `cost-log.md` you started in Hands-on 05 running all week: one row per
   session (focus, model, /cost readout, window/cap note, annotation).
2. Annotate spikes the day they happen: what drove the burn (a "read everything"
   prompt, a long debugging loop, an Opus planning block)?
3. Note any moment a 5-hour window or the weekly cap actually bit, and what you did.
4. Close the week with the end-of-week section: heaviest session and why, the mix you
   actually ran, the mix you'll run in week 2 and what changes.
