# [Week 1] Application — A week of usage, annotated

**Pedagogy:** Application · **Milestone:** Week 1 · **Due:** Sunday EOD
**Prerequisite:** Week 1 Knowledge issue complete (the log opens in Hands-on 05).

## Project

One week of real usage data. A Max subscription shows no dollar costs and no token
counts, only opaque rate limits, so the craft is relative: capture `/usage` (session and
weekly percentages, reset times) at the end of every working session, annotate the same
day, close with a model-mix decision for week 2. Token economics stops being the deck's
simulator and becomes your own patterns. Applies: Max 5x mechanics, model mix, the
measure-don't-trust posture.

## Detailed Instructions

1. Keep the `usage-log.md` you started in Hands-on 05 running all week: one row per
   session (focus, model, session %, weekly %, reset time, annotation).
2. Annotate spikes the day they happen: what moved the bar (a "read everything" prompt,
   a long debugging loop, an Opus planning block)?
3. Note any moment a window or the weekly bar actually hit its limit, and what you did
   (waited for reset, switched model, split the session).
4. Close the week with the end-of-week section: heaviest day and cause, the mix you
   actually ran, the mix you'll run in week 2 and what changes.
