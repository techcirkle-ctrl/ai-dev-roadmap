# [Week 1] Application — Loop autopsy write-up

**Pedagogy:** Application · **Milestone:** Week 1 · **Due:** Sunday EOD
**Prerequisite:** Week 1 Knowledge issue complete (the raw autopsy happens in capstone step 4).

## Project

Turn your capstone autopsy into a write-up a teammate could follow cold: a full session
on the gym repo, annotated beat by beat (gather / act / verify), with the verify signal
named and judged. The bar is transferability: someone who wasn't there reads it and can
re-run the analysis. Applies: the agentic loop as a diagnostic lens.

## Detailed Instructions

1. Start from `weeks/week-1/data/loop-autopsy-template.md` and your capstone step 4
   notes (the "relay done 2" bug session).
2. Fill every section: the session header, the stage annotation table (one row per tool
   call), the verify-signal judgement, the findings.
3. The cold-reader test: hand the write-up to another learner (swap with your audit
   partner). They must be able to say, without you explaining anything, (a) what the bug
   was, (b) which beat found the cause, (c) what closed the loop.
4. Record their verdict and fix what they couldn't follow.

## Evidence Required
