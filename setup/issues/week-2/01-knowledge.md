# [Week 2] Knowledge — Context engineering: what the model sees, at the right scope

**Pedagogy:** Knowledge · **Milestone:** Week 2 · **Due:** Sunday EOD

## Deck

`weeks/week-2/Knowledge.html` on `main`. Open it in a browser. It is self-contained and
narrated: press the voice button to have it taught aloud, or read it in silence.

Everything the week needs is in `weeks/week-2/`. Pick your target repo before Day 1 ends.

## Instructions (in order)

1. **Read through the acts.** Make sure you know the following topics covered in the deck:
   - Act 1 — The window: what occupies it, the attention budget, and why context is spend
   - Act 2 — CLAUDE.md: rules that actually get followed
   - Act 3 — Scoping: five scopes, classifying artifacts, and the two architectures
   - Act 4 — The docs layer: documents written for agents, and decision memory
   - Act 5 — Session craft: clear, compact, hand off, and recovering a poisoned session
2. **Do the hands-on exercises** embedded in each act, on your own system:
   - [ ] Hands-on 01 — /context autopsy: three readings of your real window
   - [ ] Hands-on 02 — Author + A/B one: write the file, then make it prove itself
   - [ ] Hands-on 03 — The two architectures: build both cases, catch the files loading
   - [ ] Hands-on 04 — AGENTS.md symlink: one symlink makes the layer portable
   - [ ] Hands-on 05 — Author the layer, then catch it stopping a bad edit
   - [ ] Hands-on 06 — Poison + recover: break a session on purpose, then fix it
3. **Drive every widget** and read its score line.
4. **Build the capstone project** as guided at the end of the deck: the scoped docs layer
   for a real repo or workspace, A/B'd on a five-task suite.

## Proof of Work

Press **Copy the capstone report** on the final capstone step and paste the markdown as a
comment on this issue. It must carry:

- The target repo or workspace, and why it earns the layer
- The five-task suite, and your predictions before running it
- The baseline run with no layer
- The layer you authored, with each file's scope and the argument for it
- The A/B differences, measured rather than described
- Your two verdict lines

If you lost your captures, `weeks/week-2/data/capstone-report-template.md` rebuilds the
report from your transcripts in about ten minutes.
