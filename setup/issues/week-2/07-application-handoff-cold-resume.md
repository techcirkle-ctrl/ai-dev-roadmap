# [Week 2] Application — You run out of window at 6pm and somebody else finishes the work

**Pedagogy:** Application · **Milestone:** Week 2 · **Due:** Sunday EOD
**Prerequisite:** Week 2 Knowledge issue complete.
**Capstone class:** writing for a reader with no context (capstone steps 1 and 3, compressed into one artifact)

## The Problem

Work outlives sessions. A long piece of work hits the end of a window, or the end of your
day, and everything the session learned along the way is about to evaporate: what you
already tried, what turned out to be a dead end, and the three things you would have said
out loud if anyone had asked.

Write the handoff that survives that. Then prove it by having somebody else pick the work
up cold, from your document and the repo, with you saying nothing.

The temptation is to write what you did. What the next person needs is what you learned
and what you would do next, which is a different document and a shorter one.

## Constraints

- Real work, genuinely mid-flight. A handoff written after you finished is a summary and
  teaches you nothing.
- Use `weeks/week-2/data/handoff-template.md`.
- Somebody else resumes it: another learner, or a teammate. They get your document and the
  repo, and nothing from you.
- You stay silent while they resume. Every question they ask is a gap in the handoff, and
  you record it rather than answering it.

## Evidence Required

Upload on this issue before closing:

- [ ] The handoff document, at the version you handed over
- [ ] What the person resuming had to ask you, or the fact that they asked nothing
- [ ] How far they got before they were blocked, if they were

## Success Criteria

- [ ] The work was genuinely mid-flight when you wrote the handoff
- [ ] Another person resumed it from the document and the repo alone
- [ ] Every question they asked is recorded and mapped to a section
- [ ] The revised handoff exists, with the gaps filled
- [ ] Evidence uploaded, `<name>:done` label added

problem one step at a time; it won't solve it for you. If you used it, say "guided" in
your evidence comment — using the net is fine, hiding it isn't.
