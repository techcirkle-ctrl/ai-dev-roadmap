# [Week 3] Knowledge — Harness engineering: encode it, don't repeat it

**Pedagogy:** Knowledge · **Milestone:** Week 3 · **Due:** Sunday EOD

## Deck

`weeks/week-3/Knowledge.html` on `main`. Open it in a browser. It is self-contained and
narrated: press the voice button to have it taught aloud, or read it in silence.

Everything the week needs is in `weeks/week-3/`. Pick your target repo before Day 1 ends:
one you touch weekly, where a teammate can review your PR, and where a bad edit would
actually hurt.

## Instructions (in order)

1. **Read through the acts.** Make sure you know the following topics covered in the deck:
   - Act 1 — The rule of twice: what your harness already is, and routing the repeat
   - Act 2 — Settings and permissions: three files in one order, and allowlists
   - Act 3 — Commands and hooks: saved workflows, and guarantees the model cannot skip
   - Act 4 — Skills: loaded only when relevant, and the description as the trigger
   - Act 5 — The shared harness: what you commit, and what the harness paid back
2. **Do the hands-on exercises** embedded in each act, on your own system:
   - [ ] Hands-on 01 — Mine your friction log into a ranked build list
   - [ ] Hands-on 02 — Count your approvals, then remove the ones you never refuse
   - [ ] Hands-on 03 — Write one command: turn your most-repeated prompt into one word
   - [ ] Hands-on 04 — Block a dangerous edit, then try to make it anyway
   - [ ] Hands-on 05 — Author and test one skill: it fires, and it stays quiet
   - [ ] Hands-on 06 — The cold clone test: somebody else runs your harness
3. **Drive every widget** and read its score line.
4. **Build the capstone project** as guided at the end of the deck: harness v1 in your real
   repo, raised as a pull request.

## Proof of Work

Press **Copy the capstone report** on the final capstone step and paste the markdown as a
comment on this issue. It must carry:

- The target repo, and why it earns the harness
- The five artifacts, each with its scope and the argument for that scope
- The baseline: approvals, repeated instructions and corrections, counted before you built
- The settings file and two commands
- The two hooks and one skill, with evidence they work
- The re-run and the pull request link
- Your two verdict lines

If you lost your captures, `weeks/week-3/data/capstone-report-template.md` rebuilds the
report from your transcripts in about ten minutes.
