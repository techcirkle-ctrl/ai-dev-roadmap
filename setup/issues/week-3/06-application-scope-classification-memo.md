# [Week 3] Application — Half the harness on your laptop should be in the repo, and some of it should not

**Pedagogy:** Application · **Milestone:** Week 3
**Prerequisite:** Week 3 Knowledge issue complete.
**Capstone class:** routing and scope (capstone step 1, on an accumulated pile rather than five artifacts you chose)

## The Problem

Three weeks in, you have accumulated a pile: commands, skills, settings, hooks, notes. Some
were written deliberately and some just appeared. Most of them live wherever you happened
to be when you wrote them.

Sort the whole pile. Each item belongs somewhere specific, and the placement question is
not about tidiness. A rule on one laptop protects one person. A rule in the repo protects
everyone who clones it, including the new joiner. And a machine-specific detail committed
to the repo breaks somebody else's clone on their first morning.

Write the memo a teammate could disagree with. Each item, its home, and the argument.

## Constraints

- Everything you have accumulated across weeks 1 to 3, not a selected subset.
- Use `weeks/week-3/data/scope-memo-template.md`.
- Every item gets one home and one argument. "It seemed sensible" is not an argument.
- At least one item must move as a result, and at least one must stay where it is for a
  stated reason.
- Check for anything carrying a machine-specific path, a token, or a personal preference
  that would break or annoy somebody else on clone.
- Where the cohort has shared artifacts, say which are genuinely personal and which the
  team should own.
