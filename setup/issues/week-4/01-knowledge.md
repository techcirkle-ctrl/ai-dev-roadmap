# [Week 4] Knowledge — Planning: spec-driven development

**Pedagogy:** Knowledge · **Milestone:** Week 4

## Deck

`weeks/week-4/Knowledge.html` on `main`. Open it in a browser. It is self-contained and
narrated: press the voice button to have it taught aloud, or read it in silence and lose
nothing but the examples.

Everything the week needs is in `weeks/week-4/`. Pick the feature you will ship before
Day 1 ends.

## Instructions (in order)

1. **Read through the acts.** Make sure you know the following topics covered in the deck:
   - Act 1 — Think, then act: when longer reasoning pays, and what plan mode actually blocks
   - Act 2 — The pipeline: brainstorm, spec, plan, execute, and the question each stage owns
   - Act 3 — Specs that run cold: the six parts, and driving the question count to zero
   - Act 4 — Decision memory: which decisions earn an ADR, and keeping DESIGN.md honest
   - Act 5 — Plan review: the four catch points, and the six-check rubric
2. **Do the hands-on exercises** embedded in each act, on your own system:
   - [ ] Hands-on 01 — Run one task twice (straight, then in plan mode)
   - [ ] Hands-on 02 — Brainstorm without code
   - [ ] Hands-on 03 — Write your own spec, and drive its questions to zero
   - [ ] Hands-on 04 — Write one ADR for a decision already made
   - [ ] Hands-on 05 — Review a plan, not a diff
3. **Drive every widget** and read its score line. Ten of them: the thinking dial, the
   plan mode gate, the stage sorter, the rework curve, spec anatomy, the cold-run
   simulator, the ADR threshold, the drift detector, the catch point, the rubric bench.
4. **Build the capstone project** as guided at the end of the deck: one real feature
   shipped spec-first, with its ADR.

## Proof of Work

Press **Copy the capstone report** on the final capstone step and paste the markdown as a
comment on this issue. It must carry:

- The feature, and which decision inside it was genuinely arguable
- The brainstorm brief: the problem in one sentence, at least three things cut from scope
- The spec path, plus the question count **before and after** patching, and which section
  most questions pointed at
- Six written plan-review verdicts, what you sent back, and what changed
- What the cold execution still had to ask you, and where you intervened
- The ADR path, the rejected option, and the cost of reversal
- The divergence log with each cause, and the DESIGN.md lines the feature made untrue
- Your two verdict lines

If you lost your captures, `weeks/week-4/data/capstone-report-template.md` rebuilds the
report from your artifacts in about ten minutes.
