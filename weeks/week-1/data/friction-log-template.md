# Friction log — two minutes a day, from week 1 to week 9

One line every time Claude does something wrong, or you repeat an instruction, or you
carry text between windows by hand. No analysis at capture time. This log is raw
material: week 3 turns entries into hooks and commands; week 9 merges it into your
methodology.

Format: date · what happened · which smell.

Smells: `repeat` (instruction said twice) · `paste` (manual copy between systems) ·
`wrong` (output needed correcting) · `trust` (accepted "done" with no check) ·
`context` (it didn't know something it should have).
