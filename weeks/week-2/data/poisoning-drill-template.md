# Context-poisoning drill — transcript form

Reproduce a derailed session on purpose, then fix it with hygiene alone: no new docs,
no CLAUDE.md edits, only session moves (/clear, /compact, @-refs, fresh session, a
smaller ask).

## The poison

What you injected and when: the oversized paste, the second unrelated task, the wall
of logs, the contradictory instruction. Be exact; someone else should be able to
reproduce the derailment from this section.

## Evidence of derailment

Two or three transcript moments where the session went wrong, quoted. For each: which
earlier context caused it. Add the /context reading at the point of derailment.

## The recovery

The hygiene move(s) you made and why that one. What you deliberately did not do
(restarting from zero is a move of last resort; show you considered cheaper ones).

## After

The same ask, post-recovery, with the transcript moment that shows it landing. The
/context reading now.

## The rule

One line, stated so a teammate could check you against it, that would have prevented
this poisoning.
